Method note:
Feature selection used only the training split. The approved predictors are coded as categories, so the final models use most-frequent imputation, SMOTEN for categorical oversampling, and one-hot encoding inside the imblearn Pipeline. Each GridSearchCV and cross-validation fold oversampled only its training portion. The held-out test set was never oversampled. Because outcomes are rare, model_results.csv keeps default-threshold metrics, while threshold_tuning_best_f1.csv reports the best F1 threshold on the untouched test split.
